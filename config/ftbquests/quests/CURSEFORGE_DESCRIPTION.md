# Dan Farm Create Questing

## Summary

A guided Create-focused modpack about building factories, automating food, exploring an expanded world, and turning small contraptions into ambitious production lines.

## Description

**Dan Farm Create Questing** is a Minecraft 1.21.1 modpack built around **Create**, its many add-ons, and a large collection of farming, cooking, storage, building, and exploration mods.

Begin with ordinary survival, then gradually learn how to generate rotational power, process materials, move items and fluids, automate farms, assemble trains, and build increasingly capable factories. The pack keeps Create's hands-on mechanical style at the center: production lines are meant to be visible, understandable, and satisfying to watch.

### Guided progression

The pack includes **388 quests across 19 chapters**. These quests introduce important mechanics, explain how machines and unusual items work, and reward progression with experience. The quest book covers:

- Vanilla progression and useful quality-of-life features
- Create's core kinetic machinery and automation tools
- Trains, logistics, fluid handling, storage, and portable equipment
- Advanced factories, enchantment processing, chunk loading, and specialized contraptions
- Farmer's Delight, Create Cuisine, confectionery, wine making, and numerous food add-ons
- Create add-ons focused on the Deep Dark, fantasy equipment, transportation, decoration, and more
- Optional collection and automation challenges for players looking for larger goals

The quests are designed as a guide rather than a rigid checklist. Follow the main progression when you want direction, or branch out and build at your own pace.

### Farming and food automation

Farming is more than a source of basic food. Crops can be cut, cooked, mixed, pressed, packaged, and routed through complete production lines. Farmer's Delight and its add-ons provide kitchens and a wide range of meals, while Create integrations let those recipes grow into working factories. Confectionery and winery systems add further processing chains for sweets, juices, cider, and wine.

### Factories with character

Create and its add-ons provide far more than simple ore processing. Build elevators, moving structures, hydraulic equipment, portable tools, mechanical companions, advanced storage networks, rail systems, and unusual late-game machines. Decorative mods such as Chipped, Handcrafted, Quark, Create Deco, and multiple connected-texture collections provide plenty of options for making those machines look like places instead of boxes.

### A larger world to explore

Expanded biomes, structures, Nether and End content, ocean encounters, and Deep Dark additions give you reasons to leave the factory. Exploration supplies rare ingredients, equipment, building materials, and new objectives, while JourneyMap and navigation tools help you find your way home afterward.

### Useful conveniences

The pack includes backpacks, storage drawers, Ultimine, recipe viewing, mapping, sound controls, gravestones, sleeping bags, performance improvements, and other quality-of-life tools. These reduce repetitive friction without replacing the mechanical problem-solving that makes Create enjoyable.

## Technical information

- **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
- **Mod loader:** NeoForge
- **Primary focus:** Create automation, farming, cooking, exploration, and building
- **Quest count:** 388
- **Suitable for:** Solo play or multiplayer worlds

Build a practical workshop, an enormous food factory, a rail-connected industrial town, or a machine whose purpose becomes clear only after several minutes of watching gears turn. The pack gives you the tools, explains the moving parts, and leaves the final shape of the world to you.
